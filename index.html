IT Club Activities (2026)

-5 June 2026
Plant Identification Campaign

Conducted a Plant Identification Campaign across the campus to promote environmental awareness. Students identified and documented various plant species, encouraging biodiversity conservation and the use of digital tools for learning about native plants.

-24 june 2026
Alan Turing Day Documentary Screening

Organized a screening of the documentary "The Life and Death of Alan Turing" in connection with Alan Turing Day for IT Club members. The programme highlighted Alan Turing's pioneering contributions to computer science and artificial intelligence, followed by a discussion among participants. Eight students participated in the programme.

-17 july 2026
Cyber Security Awareness Campaign

Conducted a campus-wide Cyber Security Awareness Campaign focusing on safe online practices, password security, phishing awareness, digital privacy, and responsible internet usage. Awareness materials and promotional posters were prepared as part of the campaign.

-june-july 2026
FIFA World Cup Winner Prediction Contest

Organized a FIFA World Cup Winner Prediction Contest online through the website developed by IT club member, Mr Faris Moideen to engage students during the tournament. Participants predicted the eventual FIFA World Cup champion, promoting interaction and enthusiasm among students through a fun, technology-enabled activity.
